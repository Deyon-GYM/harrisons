Polymer Science & Engineering Evaluation

Deproteinization Chemistry: Green Tires & Low-Energy Purification

Deproteinized Natural Rubber strips away the 3-5% non-rubber protein constituents encasing the *Hevea* latex particle. Beyond eliminating Type I IgE-mediated latex allergies in healthcare, protein removal completely eliminates competitive adsorption during silica compounding for low-rolling-resistance "Green Tires." Furthermore, emerging buoyancy-driven ($CO_2$ bubbling) purification drastically reduces energy consumption compared to traditional multi-stage centrifugation.

The Payne Effect in Silica Green Tires

Storage Modulus (G') vs Strain %: DPNR vs Standard NR with Silane (TESPT).

Scientific Significance: Standard NR contains proteins that compete with silane coupling agents (TESPT) for active silanol sites on silica. DPNR eliminates this interference, yielding a drastically lower Payne effect (reduced filler agglomeration), higher Bound Rubber Content (BRC), and no thermal reversion even at elevated dump temperatures (150°C).

Purification Methodology Comparison

Traditional High-Speed Centrifugation vs. Buoyancy-Driven $CO_2$ Bubbling.

Pureprena Centrifugation Benchmark 10 kWh / Ton

Uses 72-hour enzymatic hydrolysis with protease + HNS stabilizer, followed by repeated high-speed centrifugation ($10^4 \times g$). Highly effective (Nitrogen <0.12%), but energy-intensive and expensive for power-constrained grids.

Buoyancy-Driven $CO_2$ Process (Sustainable) 0.01 kWh / Ton

Saponifies lipid layers with 2% NaOH, denatures proteins with 0.1% urea/SDS, and bubbles $CO_2$ to generate ammonium carbonate. Causes rubber particles to buoyantly flocculate (1,500-4,000 nm) while dense proteins sink. Results in zero detectable nitrogen (0.000%).

⚡ Energy Reduction: Buoyancy-driven purification reduces electricity consumption by >99%, offering a carbon footprint 60-70% lower than synthetic nitrile.

Environmental, Social & Governance (ESG) Impact

EUDR Deforestation Compliance & Life Cycle Assessment (LCA)

While greenfield DPNR faces strict financial barriers, the polymer's ESG profile is exemplary. Natural rubber acts as a natural carbon sink, and low-energy DPNR offers a 60-70% reduction in greenhouse gas emissions compared to synthetic nitrile. Crucially, HML's plantation scale provides an immediate competitive moat under the **European Union Deforestation Regulation (EUDR)**.
